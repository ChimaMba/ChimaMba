Brand identity · Motion system v1.0

UTONDU moves the way a well-made sofa receives you. Things arrive with confidence, then settle slowly. Nothing snaps, nothing bounces, and nothing hurries to leave.

Principles

Settle, don't land

Every entrance starts quickly and spends most of its time easing into place. The last 40% of any move is the softest.

Build from the module

The wordmark is drawn, never faded. Stroke masks reveal the master artwork letter by letter, and the n is the U turned over: the one signature moment.

Rounded, like the letterforms

Masks, wipes and reveals take their corner radius from the O. Hard-edged geometry is reserved for the clear-space system.

Quiet palette, quiet motion

Chocolate, White Rock and Foggy Grey carry every frame. Light drifts slowly across grounds; there are no glows, flares or particles.

Transitions

Two moves between scenes

A plain 20-frame crossfade is allowed once per piece, into the end card. No slides from the side, spins, zooms or glitches.

Usage

Do

  • Reveal the master wordmark artwork with stroke masks, so every finished frame is the approved master exactly.
  • Keep ½H clear space around the finished lockup in every frame.
  • Finish every piece on the master lockup over cream or chocolate.
  • Keep supporting type in Suisse Intl Thin, Light and Regular, in caps with open tracking.

Don't

  • Bounce, overshoot or elastic-ease any brand element.
  • Fade the wordmark in as a flat image, or scale it past 110%.
  • Animate letters out of order or turn any letter other than the n.
  • Introduce colours outside the three brand tones, apart from the copper clear-space markers in construction shots.
Photography & mockups

How images move

Images only ever push in slowly, from 108% to 100% across their time on screen. A new image wipes up from the bottom over the last one on glide, in 20 frames. Full-bleed in 16:9; in 9:16 and 1:1, mockups sit on chocolate as 4:3 plates with rounded corners, so nothing is cropped away. Never use photos that carry another brand's mark.
